# HR-Analysis-PowerBI
Power BI dashboard analysing hiring and attrition trends for 13.48K employees using DAX.

## Situation
An organisation managing 13.48K employees faced rising turnover costs and operational instability. HR had no centralised view to analyse attrition or the gap between 17.18K total hires and 11.76K departures.

## Objective
- Monitor active employee counts and new hire trends
- Analyse separations by region and age group to improve retention strategies
- Understand workforce stability through average tenure across demographics

## Approach
- Built a data model and DAX measures for active headcount, new hires and separations
- Segmented the workforce by age group, pay type, region, gender and job type
- Added KPI cards (7.68K male vs 5.80K female active employees) and Region and Year slicers for filtering

## Key Findings
- **Young workforce:** 12.5K of the 17.18K new hires (about 73%) are under 30, and most under-30 employees are hourly-paid
- **Early attrition:** Under-30s make up about 70% of separations (roughly 8.3K of 11.76K), and are the largest separation group in every region
- **Regional hotspots:** North (about 2.5K) and West (about 2.4K) have the most separations
- **Tenure gap:** Average tenure is lower for women than men in every region, and South has the lowest tenure
- **Hiring mix:** Part-time hires far exceed full-time hires in every region

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query
